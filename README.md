## 👤 About Me

I'm a data analyst who bridges the gap between raw data and real business decisions. With over 7 years of hands-on experience across operations, sales, and marketing analytics, I specialize in turning complex, multi-source datasets into clear dashboards, KPI frameworks, and executive reports that people actually use.

My toolkit covers the full analytics pipeline — from writing optimized SQL queries and building ETL-supported workflows, to designing interactive Power BI and Tableau dashboards, to running statistical analysis and forecasting in Python and R. I'm equally comfortable working with structured relational databases like MySQL and BigQuery as I am wrangling unstructured or semi-structured data in MongoDB.

On the marketing side, I bring experience in campaign performance analysis, customer behavior analysis, social media analytics, and web analytics — including Google Analytics — helping teams understand what's driving engagement and where to focus effort.

What sets me apart is my ability to translate technical analysis into plain-language insights for non-technical stakeholders. Whether I'm collaborating with operations teams, briefing executives, or gathering requirements from business partners, I keep the focus on decisions, not just data.

I'm currently pursuing my **M.S. in Computer Science** (Data Science and AI concentrations) at the University of the Potomac, deepening my foundations in machine learning, modeling, and AI-driven analytics.
