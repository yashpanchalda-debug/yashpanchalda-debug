Results-driven **Data Analyst** with a track record of turning complex operational, sales, and customer data into executive-ready insights that directly influence business decisions — including reporting delivered straight to C-suite leadership (CEO, COO, Director, Managing Director).

Proven strength across three high-demand analytics domains:

- 📊 **Data Analytics & BI** — SQL, Python, R, Power BI, Tableau, Excel
- 🏥 **Healthcare & Operational Analytics** — KPI tracking, data quality, performance reporting, process analysis
- 📣 **Marketing Analytics** — Customer insights, campaign & social media analytics, business growth analysis
